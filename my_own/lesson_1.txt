AI, ML, Deep Learning, aur Generative AI mein kya farq hai

Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, aur LLMs — Asal Mein Kya Hain?

1   Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Simple definition: Machines ko is tarah program karna ke woh aise kaam karein jo normally insaan ki "intelligence" maangta hai.

2  Machine Learning (ML)

Simple definition: AI ka woh subset jahan hum machine ko explicitly rules nahi likhte — balke hum usse data se seekhne dete hain.

3 Deep Learning

Simple definition: Machine Learning ka woh subset jo Neural Networks use karta hai — especially deep (bahut saari layers wali) neural networks.

4  Generative AI

Simple definition: AI jo naya content create kar sake — text, image, audio, video, code.

Generative AI = Deep Learning ka ek powerful application

Part 4: Parameters aur Model Weights

Parameters = Model ke andar ke numbers

Jab hum kehte hain "GPT-4 has 1.8 trillion parameters" ya "Llama 3 is a 70B model" — to yeh in numbers ki count hai.

Yeh numbers training ke doran adjust hote hain taake model better predictions kare.

Analogy: Socho ek expert doctor ke dimaag mein medical knowledge store hai. Us knowledge ko agar numbers mein express karein — to woh uske "parameters" hain. Jitna zyada experience, utne better parameters.


Transformer Architecture — Brief Overview

2017 mein Google ne ek paper publish kiya: "Attention Is All You Need"

Is paper ne ek naya architecture introduce kiya: Transformer

Yeh architecture itna powerful nikla ke aaj ke almost sare LLMs isi par based hain — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, sab.

Transformer ka key innovation: Attention Mechanism

Jab model text process karta hai, to woh har word ko equally nahi dekhta. Woh attention deta hai — yani kuch words zyada important hain context ke liye.
ransformer AI ke dimagh ka woh naya design (naksha) hai jis ne AI ko poora jumla (sentence) ek saath padhne aur har lafz ka asli matlab (context) samajhne ki taqat di, aur isi wajah se aaj ChatGPT, Claude, aur Gemini itne samajhdar hain.
• Transformer. AI ke dimagh ka woh modern design jo poore text ko ek saath padhta hai.
• Attention. AI ki woh salahiyat jis se woh jumle ke ahem alfaaz par focus karke un ka aapas mein rishta aur asli matlab samajhta hai.

Part 6: Training vs Inference
Training aur Inference AI ki zindagi ke do alag-alag marhale (stages) hain: "Training" ka matlab hai AI ko padha-likha kar tayyar karna (Imtehan ki tayyari), aur "Inference" ka matlab hai us tayyar-shuda AI se asli kaam lena ya sawalon ke jawab poochhna (Imtehan dena).

# 📝 J. Lesson Summary
- **Structured Output (JSON):** Software APIs ke sath baat karne ke liye lazmi hai.
- **Pydantic:** Schema validation aur type safety provide karta hai.
- **Function Calling:** LLM faisla karta hai aur arguments generate karta hai; Python application function ko physically execute karti hai.
